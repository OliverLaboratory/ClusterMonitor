    ▼Reserve 💬 #compute
    Reservations are a coordination board, not a scheduler. They don't block anyone from using the hardware — they just let you announce "I plan to use N GPUs / CPUs on this box for the next few hours" so others know not to fill it up first. Handy before long training runs or weekend experiments.
    • Over-capacity rows are highlighted orange with a ⚠ badge — the app warns but still lets you submit.
    • Upcoming (start date in the future) rows are tinted blue and show "starts in…".
    • Only you can cancel or edit your own reservations.
    • Anyone with an account can see everyone's reservations.
    ▼CO₂ Leaderboard
    How is this computed? Every time the dashboard polls a server, we read each GPU's power.draw in watts and multiply by elapsed seconds to get energy in kWh. Each GPU's energy is split among its processes by their GPU-memory share and attributed to the user who owns them. Multiply by 0.35 kg CO₂ / kWh (TVA grid average) to get emissions. Accounting runs only while someone has the dashboard open, so these totals are a lower bound on reality.
